= App Info, Logs & Settings

The *⋮* at the end of the Copilot toolbar opens its menu. The top of the menu shows your Vaadin account and messages, such as tips for your development setup and the release notes of a new version. Below them is what's about your whole application, or Copilot itself, rather than one view. Look here for its versions and log, feature flags, signing in as one of its users, feedback and help, and Copilot's settings.
